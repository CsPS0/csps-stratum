# Gaming & Entertainment Stratum

Game clients, libraries, multi-store launchers, and specialized hardware tuning utilities.

---

- **Steam Games**:
  - *NTE: Neverness to Everness* (Open World RPG)
  - *THE FINALS* (Multiplayer FPS)
  - *Battlefield™ 6* (Tactical FPS)
  - *Counter-Strike 2* (Competitive Tactical Shooter)
  - *Bodycam* (Photorealistic Shooter)
  - *Tom Clancy's Rainbow Six Siege* (Tactical CQC Shooter)
  - *PEAK* (Climbing / Action)
  - *Turbo Overkill* (Cyberpunk Retro FPS)
  - *STRAFTAT* (1v1 Arena Shooter)
  - *The Forest* (Survival Horror)
  - *Silly Linguine Cat Simulator Deluxe Online* (Casual Physics)

  - **Heroic Games Launcher**: Epic Games library (*Fortnite*)
  - **HoYoPlay**: *Genshin Impact*
  - **Prism Launcher**: Minecraft
  - **Xbox App**: PC Game Pass
  - **Ubisoft Connect**
  - **Roblox**

---

## Development & Utility Stacks

- **Game Engine**: Godot Engine 4.x
- **Mobile Capture**: scrcpy (low-latency device mirroring)
- **Hardware Telemetry & Diagnostics**: HWiNFO64, OCCT, DDU
- **Peripherals**: Logitech G HUB, Mouse sensitivity converters

---

## Included Declarations

- [`steam.yaml`](steam.yaml): Steam game catalog, AppIDs, and categories.
- [`launchers.yaml`](launchers.yaml): Multi-store manifests (Heroic, HoYoPlay, Prism Launcher, Xbox, Ubisoft).
- [`tools.yaml`](tools.yaml): Hardware monitoring, engine tools, and peripheral tuning.
